Meet the team

Shekayla Shears

Founder, speaker, author and mentor

Shekayla is an executive consultant, public speaker and author. She joined Ambit Energy in 2020 and quickly became known as #TheEnergyLady, helping families and entrepreneurs understand residual income and multiple streams of revenue.

Her work ethic and heart for people earned her the nickname “The Shark” and a fast track into leadership. Today she leads Team Ambit & Beyond and speaks on understanding people, strengthening teams and building legacies.

She is the author of Still Building and builds everything she does on faith, purpose, people and progress.

Calvin and Verna Mitchell

The Wise Group

Senior Consultant Calvin Mitchell and his wife Verna Mitchell bring a wealth of wisdom and experience to the team. Specializing in personal development, teamwork and relationship building, they help entrepreneurs find balance in their business while strengthening connections within their teams.

Calvin and Verna are passionate about teaching the power of relationships, showing you how to be personable with your team, build trust and foster long-term growth. Their coaching style ensures that every individual receives the guidance needed to move in the right direction and reach the next level of success.

Looking for personal development from Calvin and Verna? Join our team.

Velma King

Team partner · Trainer & project manager

Velma works alongside Shekayla to support clients, develop leaders and build the team. As a trainer and project manager, she coordinates training sessions and teaches the foundational skills of entrepreneurship, team building and business growth.

She brings focus, professionalism and care to helping team members gain confidence and put what they learn into action.

Treajour Williams

Emerging team leader · Gen Z connection and social media

Treajour is an up-and-coming leader with Ambit Energy who brings fresh ideas, enthusiasm and a passion for helping the next generation grow. Her focus is connecting Gen Z with opportunities in the energy sector through social media, authentic relationships and creative ways to build a business.

Drawing from the strategies that helped her earn her own promotion, Treajour shares practical ideas to help young entrepreneurs stay connected, take action and enjoy the process. She believes building a business should be engaging and rewarding, and she brings that energy to helping others move forward with confidence.
